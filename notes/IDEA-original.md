# Too Fast

##Description
Too fast is a Street race car board game where up to 4 players start at the starting line, and the game ends when the first person passes the finish line. There is multiple routes to get to finish. The streets will resemble the city of Tokyo roots can intersect that Shibuya station each space can have one of the following.:
	- collect  X reputation points
	- pick up a boost card
	- pick up an obstacle card
	- gas station
	- speed trap where you have to pull over and pay reputation points
	- other Tokyo landmarks where we can do something fun
	- other ideas on spaces, you can leave the oil slick that if another player lands on it they have to go back four spaces to get oil slick by picking up a boost card that has that ability, like:
		- Certain Tiles give you a flat tire allows you to re-roll for a minus dice movement
		- Certain tiles allow advancements like traffic break Anna allows you to
