The manual includes setup, turn order, movement, upgrades and their costs, Fuel, Heat, police encounters, Street Duels, card rules, optional game modes, and a quick-reference section.

I've used a dark navy, electric cyan, and hot-pink racing theme with high-contrast text. This is a clean first edition; we can make the next version more illustrated with Tokyo street-racing graphics and dashboard-style visual elements.
How to print and assemble
1. Print the fold-and-staple PDF, double-sided, on US Letter paper.
2. Choose Landscape, Actual Size (100%), and Flip on Short Edge.
3. Stack the four sheets in printed order and fold them together down the middle.
4. Staple twice along the center fold.
The pages are already arranged in booklet order, so no manual page rearrangement is needed.
