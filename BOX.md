# Too Fast — Box Copy (Draft v0.3)

## Front of the box

**TOO FAST**
*Street racing through the heart of Tokyo*

> **Go fast. Get famous. Don't get caught.**

2–4 players · Ages 10+ · 30–45 minutes

---

## Back of the box

**Midnight in Tokyo. The streets are empty, the neon is bright, and the race is on.**

Race from the Odaiba waterfront, through the chaos of Shibuya Crossing, to the finish at Tokyo Tower. The first car there wins. But every choice has a cost.

**Choose your route.** Blast down the Wangan Expressway, where the police are waiting. Cruise Ginza Avenue's bright lights. Or slip through the twisting Back Alleys, slow but full of street cred.

**Build your ride.** Earn Reputation to bolt on a Turbo, Racing Tires, a Police Scanner or a shot of Nitrous. Slide each upgrade into your dashboard and watch your car get faster.

**Watch your gauges.** Your fuel drops every turn, so plan your pit stops or push your car back to the last gas station. Drive too hard and your Heat climbs toward the red line. Hit it and you're Busted.

**Play dirty.** Drop oil slicks in front of rivals, hack the traffic lights in Akihabara, block narrow streets, and settle scores in Street Duels.

**Every race is different.** Shuffled Boost and Obstacle cards bring flat tires, police checkpoints, bribes, shortcuts and empty streets at just the right moment (or the worst one).

*Easy to learn, fast to play, and full of close finishes.*

**Contents:** game board · 4 race cars · 4 player dashboards with spinning fuel and Heat dials · 25 Boost cards · 25 Obstacle cards · 16 Upgrade cards · Reputation chips · 6 oil slick tokens · dice · rulebook
